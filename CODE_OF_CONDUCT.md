# Code of Conduct

Rantakit welcomes everyone. In issues, pull requests, and anywhere else the project lives:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Don't share anyone's personal data or documents.

If someone breaks these rules, email **moezrd.dev@gmail.com**. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who keeps breaking them.
